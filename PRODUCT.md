# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- People evaluating Marcos "H0wZy" Junior for work: a client who wants a problem solved, or a recruiter or partner checking what he can do.
- They arrive to see real projects, understand his skills, and find a way to reach him and hire the service.

## Product Purpose

howzysolutions.com is three things at once:
- a portfolio of the projects he builds
- a showcase of the services anyone can hire him for, with every contact route in view
- the site of his future company (a CNPJ is planned from here on)

Success means a visitor can see the work, trust the skills, and contact him to hire a service without hunting for how.

## Positioning

"howzy" is the owner's nickname and "solutions" means solving problems: the company sells solved problems, proven by the projects shown, built by one named developer and not by a faceless agency.

## Operating Context

- Static, prerendered React and TypeScript site. Every route ships as real HTML.
- Deployed as a Cloudflare assets-only Worker.
- Existing routes: home, work index and project pages, showcase and live showcase, CV, MCP and legal pages.
- auth.howzysolutions.com (Keycloak, owner-only sign-in for his tools) belongs to the same brand.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The domain `howzysolutions.com` and the name `howzysolutions` stay.
- English and Brazilian Portuguese on every route.
- The CV stays. It is where a visitor finds his skills.
- Contact information and ways to hire him are first-class content, not a footer afterthought.
- Open: the company's legal registration (CNPJ) is not done yet. Do not claim a registered company, clients, prices or numbers he has not given.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: howzysolutions, from the nickname "howzy".
- The owner refuses a look that reads as generated by AI ("AI slop"), including fonts and patterns that became AI defaults (he rejected Instrument Serif on 2026-11-22).
- vvv's Mythic Dither world (mythological engravings, one colour per app) belongs to the vvv product family. Whether any of it carries into the parent brand is an open decision for the rebrand.

## Evidence on Hand

- The real projects and the CV content already on the site (`src/pages/`, `specs/002-portfolio-craft-pass/`).
- No testimonials, client list, case metrics or pricing exist. Never invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work first, because the projects are the proof.
2. Make hiring obvious, with a clear route to contact on every page.
3. Be one real person's company, specific and human, never generic.
4. Make both languages first-class: nothing exists in one language only.
